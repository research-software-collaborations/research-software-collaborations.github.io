project_goal: >
    The Detector Characterization (DetChar) working group plays a central role in monitoring LIGO
    detector operation and improving astrophysical sensitivity by identifying instrumental and
    environmental artifacts that corrupt the gravitational-wave strain channel. With LIGO-India
    expected to begin operations around 2030, it is critical to build local expertise in DetChar
    software tools.

    During this visit, the work will focus on two objectives. First, software will be developed
    to collect cumulative statistics of h-veto results and to run h-veto on custom time periods
    and channel lists. These tools will be used to identify sources of noise transients correlated
    with the gravitational-wave strain during the first Intermediate Run (IR1), in collaboration
    with on-site staff and the LSU DetChar group. Second, usage statistics for the LIGO Summary
    Pages will be collected and analysed to study the architecture of the existing system and
    propose a phased plan for creating equivalent pages for LIGO-India.

    The results will be documented and shared with the LIGO Scientific Collaboration, directly
    supporting the DetChar infrastructure needed for LIGO-India commissioning.
