project_goal: >
    The collaboration will produce benefits for both institutions.
    
    For LLO, the visit provides an opportunity to review and plan modernization of an important
    operational software system with additional software engineering support from collaborators who
    will continue contributing to the project over the long term.

    For IUCAA, the visit will establish the technical foundation needed to begin local development of
    the Inventory Control System. The knowledge gained will enable the IUCAA team to mentor
    undergraduate developers, establish software engineering best practices, and build long-term local
    expertise.

    More broadly, the collaboration will establish a common development model for the Inventory
    Control System. By maintaining a shared software platform, future improvements developed by
    either laboratory can be more easily adopted by the other, reducing duplicated effort while
    improving software quality, maintainability, and long-term sustainability.
